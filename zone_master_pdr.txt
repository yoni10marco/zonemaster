Product Requirements Document (PRD) – Zone Master
1. Vision & Objective
Zone Master is a web application designed for endurance athletes and triathletes (with a focus on Half Ironman and Ironman distances). It serves as a comprehensive tool for planning, tracking, and analyzing training routines. The platform is built around a "user-first" philosophy, granting athletes full manual control over their training calendar while providing a supportive wrapper of smart analytics, data synchronization, and on-demand AI coaching.

2. Target Audience
Primary: Amateur and age-group triathletes preparing for specific target races (e.g., Israman 70.3 in January) who need a clean, structured environment to manage their weekly training load.

Secondary: General endurance sports enthusiasts (runners, cyclists, swimmers) looking for a unified platform to log activities and consult with an intelligent assistant.

3. User Authentication & Onboarding
Standard Authentication: The system will utilize standard email and password registration. Social/Google login will not be included in the initial MVP.

Profile Setup: Upon registration, users define their current fitness level, primary discipline, and overarching goals (e.g., target race date and distance).

Optional Strava Integration: A dedicated settings area allows users to securely authorize their Strava account via OAuth for automated workout synchronization.

4. Core Features
4.1 Manual-First Workout Scheduling
Blank Canvas Calendar: A clean weekly and monthly calendar view. The schedule starts entirely empty, ensuring the user maintains full autonomy over their routine.

Custom Workout Blocks: Users manually create training blocks for swimming, cycling, running, or strength conditioning. Blocks include target duration, distance, and intensity (e.g., specifying a 25km Zone 2 long run versus a Zone 3-4 speed interval session).

Drag & Drop Interface: Seamlessly drag and drop scheduled workouts between days to quickly adjust to shifting personal schedules or weather constraints.

4.2 Tracking & Performance Sync
Automated Syncing: For connected Strava accounts, completed activities (distance, time, average pace/power, heart rate) are automatically pulled and matched against the planned workout for that day.

Manual Entry (Fallback): Users can manually log completed workouts if they choose not to use integrations or if a session fails to sync (e.g., an indoor virtual ride on Zwift using a Wahoo KICKR). Required inputs include duration, distance, and a subjective Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE on a scale of 1-10).

Weekly Dashboard: Visual charts comparing planned versus actual training volume (hours and kilometers) across all disciplines to track progressive load accurately.

4.3 On-Demand AI Coach
Opt-In Smart Suggestions: A dedicated "Plan My Week" or "Suggest Workout" button. The AI does not auto-populate the calendar; instead, upon request, it analyzes the current training load and time left until race day, proposing specific workouts that the user must explicitly approve before they are added to the calendar.

Conversational AI Assistant: A built-in chat interface with an AI that understands the user's logged history, target race, and upcoming plans.

Example Queries:

"I missed yesterday's swim; how should I adjust the rest of my week?"

"What's a good energy gel nutrition strategy for tomorrow's 3-hour ride?"

"Suggest a demanding indoor interval session for my smart trainer."

4.4 Planning Notifications & Engagement
Weekly Planning Alerts: Automated push notifications or emails sent at a fixed time at the start of the week (e.g., Sunday at 08:00 AM), reminding the user to sit down and map out their upcoming schedule.

Weekly Review: The notification includes a brief, motivating summary of the past week’s completion rate (e.g., "You hit 85% of your planned volume last week. Let's plan the next block!").

5. Technology Stack & Architecture
To ensure a scalable, fast, and maintainable SaaS application, the following stack is outlined:

Frontend: Built with Next.js for optimized performance, routing, and SEO benefits. Styled using Tailwind CSS for a highly responsive and modern UI.

Backend & Database: Powered by Supabase (PostgreSQL) to efficiently handle relational data (users, scheduled workouts, metrics) alongside rapid API endpoints.

Authentication: Managed via BetterAuth (or native Supabase Auth) for secure, standard email/password credential management.

AI Integration: Utilizing LLM APIs (such as Gemini or OpenAI) combined with backend system prompts that feed the user's recent training data into the AI's context window for highly personalized coaching responses.

6. High-Level Database Schema
Users: User ID, Email, Hashed Password, Target Race Date, Current Fitness Level.

Integrations: Strava OAuth tokens (for opted-in users).

Planned_Workouts: Scheduled calendar entries (Target Date, Discipline, Planned Duration, Planned Distance, Target Zone/Intensity).

Completed_Workouts: Executed sessions (Source [Manual/Strava], Execution Date, Actual Duration, Actual Distance, RPE).

AI_Chat_History: Conversation logs with the AI coach to maintain context across different sessions.
